# Parametric Memory Overrides Retrieved Scientific Passages

Authors: N. Iyer. Critique of Grounded Generation, 2025.

We tested whether showing retrieved passages changes what a model asserts on scientific questions. The corpus, questions, and retrieved sentences were fixed in advance. For each question the prompt included the passages that a search step had returned, including at least one passage that contradicted a common textbook formulation.

Supplying retrieved passages did not reduce the rate of unsupported claims. The model answered from parametric memory even when a contradicting passage was in the prompt. In the paired comparison the unsupported-claim rate was 29 percent with passages and 27 percent without them, a difference small enough to treat as no improvement. When we asked the model to quote the passage it had used, it often quoted a sentence that did not entail the claim, or it quoted nothing and still kept the claim.

This result disagrees with reports that retrieval itself makes scientific answers more faithful. In our setup the model preferred a familiar explanation stored in its parameters over the passage that was placed in the context window. Until a system can show that a claim was forced to follow a retrieved sentence, adding a search step should not be described as fact-checking.
