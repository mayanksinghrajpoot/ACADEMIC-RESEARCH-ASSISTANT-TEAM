# Short Quotes Leave Room for Scope Slips

Authors: Elena Reyes. Methods Note on Citation Length, 2024.

A quote gate accepts a claim when a copied span from the cited passage is present. We asked whether the length of that span changes how often the claim still overreaches the passage. The same questions and the same retrieved passages were used. Only the minimum quote length changed.

When the required quote was 20 characters, unsupported claims that survived the gate were mostly scope slips. The quote was real and the claim added a population, a year, or a comparison the passage did not make. When the required quote was a full sentence, those slips fell, and a new failure appeared: the model copied the sentence and then attached a second clause the sentence did not support.

Short quotes are easy to satisfy and weak as evidence. A longer quote makes the copy check stricter, and it does not by itself prove that the whole claim follows from the passage. The gate still needs a reader, or a second check, for the words that sit outside the quote.
